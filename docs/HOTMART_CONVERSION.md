# Converting Hotmart / course-platform sellers → RASHMAT

Target: coaches who already sell **video courses** on Hotmart, Eduzz, Kiwify, Monetizze, Teachable, Gumroad, etc.

They are not “new creators.” They already have catalog, audience, and checkout. What they lack is **structured execution + progress** — and that is the RASHMAT wedge.

---

## Their world today

| What they have | What students experience |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Modules of long videos | Binge / abandon |
| Checkout + delivery page | “I bought it… now what?” |
| Comments / WhatsApp support | Chaos, no completion data |
| Launch spikes | Flat retention after week 2 |

**Pitch in one line:**  
Hotmart sells the download. RASHMAT sells the **camp** — Week → Day → Session → Drill → done.

Or: *Don’t just sell videos. Sell a program athletes finish.*

---

## Who to approach first

Ideal Hotmart/course sellers for RASHMAT:

- BJJ / No-Gi / wrestling / MMA **technique courses** (not generic fitness)
- Already teaching in modules (easy to map to weeks)
- 1k–100k followers or a warm email list
- Frustrated that buyers don’t finish / don’t “get results”
- Open to a **companion app** without ripping Hotmart checkout on day one

Avoid first wave: pure “passive income” gurus with no mat credibility, or 40-hour mega-libraries with no narrative.

---

## Conversion offer (do not ask them to leave Hotmart yet)

> Keep selling on Hotmart. We turn your best course into a **4–6 week training camp** on RASHMAT. Buyers get a clear schedule; you see who completes.  
> We help structure it. You bring the videos you already filmed.

**Phases:**

1. **Companion** — Hotmart checkout stays; RASHMAT is the training surface (access code / “enrolled” manually or via link).
2. **Primary** — later: sell inside RASHMAT when payments exist.

Do not lead with “migrate off Hotmart.” Lead with **completion and reputation**.

---

## Mapping: Hotmart course → RASHMAT program

| Hotmart | RASHMAT |
|---------|---------|
| Product / course | Program |
| Module 1, 2, 3… | Week 1, 2, 3… (or group modules into weeks) |
| Lesson / class | Session (Day) |
| Chapter / technique clip | Drill |
| Bonus PDF | Session notes / description |
| Cover image | Program cover (Storage upload) |

### Heuristics

- If they have 20 long lessons → pick **one system** (e.g. guard retention) and make a **6-week camp**, not the whole catalog.
- Cap at 3–4 sessions/week — athletes won’t finish daily mega dumps.
- First drill of each session = the “key technique”; later drills = positional / live rounds.
- Rename “Aula 07” → `W2D1 · Knee shield retention`.

Studio tools that help: **wizard templates**, **auto-schedule**, **duplicate day**, **starter block**.

## 10-day conversion playbook (per seller)

### Day 0 — Recruit
DM / email with their actual product name:

> Saw your [Course X] on Hotmart. Buyers get hours of video — few finish.  
> We help turn the core system into a 6-week camp on RASHMAT (schedule + progress).  
> Hotmart can stay for checkout. 20-min call?

### Day 1 — Call
- Confirm sport + which **one** course to convert first  
- Ask for: module list, ideal days/week, cover photo from mats  
- Show Studio tour + Students progress screen  
- Agree: live draft in ≤10 days  

### Day 2–4 — Structure (you / Studio)
- Create program from closest template  
- Map modules → weeks/days  
- Upload **cover** via Storage  
- Paste Mux IDs or placeholders per drill  
- Keep draft  

### Day 5 — Seller review
- CMS walkthrough: rename, reorder, duplicate  
- Fix language: sessions/drills/rounds — not “aulas/módulos” in the athlete UI  

### Day 6–7 — Athlete smoke test
- 1–2 test accounts complete sessions  
- Show them Students %  

### Day 8 — Soft launch to their list
- Email/story: “New: follow Course X as a 6-week camp — don’t just watch.”  
- Link to app / enroll path  

### Day 9–10 — Debrief
- Completion after 14 days  
- Ask for intro to 1 peer who also sells on Hotmart

## Objection handling

| Objection | Reply |
|-----------|--------|
| “I already sell on Hotmart” | Keep Hotmart. RASHMAT is the training layer so students finish. |
| “I don’t have time to rebuild” | We structure it. You send the outline + videos. |
| “My course is 30 hours” | We productize **one system** as a camp. Rest stays on Hotmart. |
| “Will I lose sales?” | You’re adding a reason to buy: progress, not another dump of MP4s. |
| “App fatigue” | Athletes already train; a session plan beats another login to binge. |

---

## Messaging kit

**Headline:** Don’t just watch. Progress.  
**Sub:** Turn your Hotmart course into a camp athletes actually finish.  
**Proof:** Creator dashboard — enrollments, % complete, drop-off day.  
**CTA:** Book a structuring call / Open Studio.

Avoid: “marketplace,” “disrupt Hotmart,” “replace your funnel.”

---

## Success metrics (per converted seller)

- Program published (or shared draft to their buyers)  
- ≥20 enrollments in 30 days **or** ≥30% of enrolled finish ≥3 sessions  
- Seller posts once about the camp  

If Hotmart sellers won’t structure even with white-glove, the problem isn’t Storage or CMS — it’s positioning. Iterate the offer before adding features.
