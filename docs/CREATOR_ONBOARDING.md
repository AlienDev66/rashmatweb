# Creator onboarding playbook (first 5–10)

White-glove path to seed the RASHMAT marketplace with **BJJ / No-Gi / grappling** creators first.

Thesis to protect: **creator builds program → athlete executes → RASHMAT tracks → creator sees evolution.**

---

## Goal

Ship **5–10 real programs** athletes can complete end-to-end — not a giant catalog.

Creators do **not** need to be Gordon Ryan. Prefer:

- Local black belts / respected coaches
- Competitors with 10k–100k followers
- People who already teach (gym classes, online notes, IG breakdowns)

---

## Offer you make them

> “Send us your videos + the system in your head. We turn it into Week → Day → Session → Drill on RASHMAT. You publish. Athletes follow. You see who finishes.”

You (or Studio ops) do the structuring. They do knowledge + footage.

---

## 7-day white-glove flow

### Day 0 — Recruit
- Short DM / call (15 min): show Studio + one sample program
- Ask for: sport focus, 4–6 week theme, 2–3 sessions/week, raw videos or Mux-ready clips later
- Promise: first program live in ≤7 days with your help

### Day 1 — Kickoff (30–45 min)
- Activate creator in Studio
- Run the **in-app tour** (Dashboard → Programs → Wizard → CMS → Students)
- Pick a template in the wizard (BJJ Fundamentals / No-Gi / blank)
- Agree naming: e.g. `6 Weeks — Guard Retention System`

### Day 2–3 — Structure (you)
- Confirm weeks × days/week in wizard → auto-schedule
- Map their curriculum onto days (W1D1 Frames, W1D2 Concepts…)
- Paste Mux IDs when available (upload pipeline later)
- Fill missing days with **Auto-fill** if they expand the block

### Day 4 — Creator review
- Walk CMS together: edit drill names, reorder, duplicate a strong day
- Publish as **draft** first; share athlete test account

### Day 5 — Athlete smoke test
- Enroll a test athlete (you or a friend)
- Complete 1–2 sessions on mobile
- Confirm Students page shows progress

### Day 6 — Soft publish
- Publish program
- Creator posts 1 story/reel: “Don’t just watch. Progress.” + link

### Day 7 — Debrief
- What blocked them? (video, time, UX)
- Ask for intro to 1 peer coach
- Schedule monthly check-in on completion %

---

## What you collect from each creator

1. Program title + 2-sentence promise  
2. Weeks, days/week, level  
3. Outline of each day (even bullet notes)  
4. Videos per drill (or “coming later” placeholders)  
5. Cover image (mat / training — not gym lifts)  
6. Consent to show them as early creator

---

## Success metrics (validate the two hypotheses)

| Hypothesis | Signal in 30 days |
|------------|-------------------|
| Creators publish | ≥5 programs published from real coaches |
| Athletes complete | ≥30% of enrolled athletes finish ≥3 sessions |

If neither moves, stop adding features — fix onboarding and content.

---

## Do / Don’t

**Do**
- Grappling-first GTM
- Build their first program with them
- Measure completion, not downloads
- Keep language: sessions, drills, rounds, camps

**Don’t**
- Expand to every combat sport on day one
- Build gym admin (check-in, dues, CRM)
- Launch paid ads before 5 live programs
- Let creators face a blank CMS alone
